# Golden Thread workspace update

## What it is
A social-listening research and content studio. Research programs collect evidence and produce trends; the team approves trends and channels make content from them.

## Who uses it
Super Admins manage workspaces and account membership. Workspace Admins manage their own team and research. Editors develop trends and content. Channel Owners choose and use approved trends for their channel.

## Where and when
The existing Mac app and shared browser app, normally on a laptop or desktop; the browser must also work at phone width.

## What they come to do
Understand which workspace they are in, find relevant research and trends, and create assets for their channel. Add and configure workspaces and channel image examples when authorized.

## What must stay true
Workspaces and channels keep their data separate. Existing work and sign-in identities survive. Trend approval is separate from a channel choosing to use a trend. Paid operations require the existing explicit confirmation.

## What we have
The running Golden Thread Mac app, its established Golden Thread/Semrush branding, shared React renderer, CommonJS application services, role policy, channel adoption model and CSS tokens. Adobe Firefly's saved draft setup is available under docs/feedback; its research is not run automatically.
