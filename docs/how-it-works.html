From what marketers are saying right now to channel-ready content, for the Semrush marketing team.

The loop · research to channel-ready content
01

Collect

  • Apify collects posts from saved X accounts, LinkedIn profiles and subreddits.
  • Programs run on demand or on a schedule; every run is capped and priced first.
Research programs · Quick scans
02

Find trends

  • AI reads the posts in batches and names clear trends.
  • The Trend tracker follows each one across runs: new, rising, steady, fading.
Trends · Trend board · Sheets
03

Decide

  • The team says Yes or Skip, with a note and the channels it suits.
  • Approval rules and Monday.com statuses can decide too.
Needs your OK · Inbox · Monday
04

Write

  • Approved trends get a trends report and an editorial toolkit, evidence linked.
  • Teammates co-edit in a minimal editor, leave notes, keep every version.
Reports · Library · Prompts
05

Make

  • One report becomes every channel asset, from blog and newsletter to paid, PR and social.
  • Pictures and short videos follow the brand reference images.
Assets · Make channel assets
06

Ship

  • Assets move Draft → In review → Approved → Scheduled → Published.
  • Reports land in Google Drive; trends sync to Monday.com.
Calendar · Analytics · Drive
